Plainly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Plainly is a food scanner that shows what is in your food and how we grade it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What stays on your phone

Your profile (name, diet and allergen choices), Pantry, scan history, Strictness setting and preferences are stored on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not keep copies of this data on our servers.

What leaves your phone

1. Ask messages. When you use Ask, your message and the product and ingredient information it relates to are sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Anthropic, our AI provider, to write a reply. Ask messages are not used to identify you. If you switch on profile sharing in Ask, your diet and allergen choices are included to personalise the answer. Your name is never sent.

2. Food lookups. When you scan a barcode or search a food, we send the barcode or search text to public food databases (Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central) to get product data.

3. Subscriptions. Purchases are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and subscription status so we can unlock Premium. We never see your payment card.

4. Device identifier. Our server uses a random install ID to apply daily message limits and prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and read ingredient lists. Images are processed to read text and are not stored or sent to us.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use advertising SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Data on your phone is deleted when you delete the app. Ask messages are not stored by us beyond what is needed to deliver a reply and apply rate limits. To ask about data held by RevenueCat or our server, or to request deletion, email us at the address below.

Children

Plainly is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
